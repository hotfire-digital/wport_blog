This page explains how to delete your **WPORT 職航站** account. It applies to job seeker accounts on the WPORT 職航站 app (iOS and Android) and on the website wport.me. Both use the same account, so you only need to do it once, in either place.

Deleting your account also deletes your profile, resumes, application history and saved jobs. **This cannot be undone.**

## Before you start: do you still own a company?

If your account is also the owner of a company on WPORT, **transfer the company to another member or delete the company first**. Only then can you delete your personal account.

## Delete your account in the app (iOS and Android)

1. After signing in, tap the **Settings** icon in the bottom right corner
2. Tap **Account Security**
3. Tap **Delete Account** and follow the on-screen confirmation

> Click any image to zoom in. Red boxes show where to tap, and the numbers match the steps above.

## Delete your account on the website

1. Sign in at [wport.me](https://wport.me) and click your **profile picture** in the top right corner
2. Choose **Account Settings** from the drop-down menu
3. At the bottom of the page, click **Delete Account**
4. Type `DELETE`, then click the confirm button

You can also go straight to the [Account Settings page](https://wport.me/user/account-setting) after signing in.
